A simple example

A short page to try out markmyword. Every word here is invented.

This document renders exactly as written — markmyword never rewrites your markup, it only overlays your edits on top.

Switch to Suggesting, highlight a phrase, and propose a rewrite. Or leave a comment on any element, even a link like this one.

When you're happy, hit Download to get the reviewed HTML back — a clean file with your edits baked in.

That's the whole idea: review a real page as a real page, then take the result with you.
